# Dependencies and reuse

The research code, model inputs and numerical results are provided as a research
record. No blanket open-source or data-reuse licence has been assigned in this
local preparation. Please contact the repository owner before reuse beyond the
permissions provided by GitHub's terms and applicable law.

EnergyPlus, Shapely, NumPy and Matplotlib are separately maintained software with
their own licences. Their binaries and source distributions are not bundled.
Weather data are obtained separately from the linked EnergyPlus weather archive.
Downloading third-party software or weather data does not change their terms.

The Python standard library is sufficient for the numerical reproduction and
test commands. `requirements.txt` lists the additional dependencies for geometry
generation and plotting. Repository-generated charts are derived from the
included numerical and geometric data, not copied journal figures.
